How the silence cutter decides what to cut

It measures the loudness of your audio every 10 milliseconds and learns the level of your room's background noise from the recording itself, so it works for a quiet studio and a noisy kitchen alike. Anything that stays near that background level for long enough is a pause.

The start and end of the video are trimmed too, so the clip begins on your first word. Cuts land in the quietest moment nearby and the sound fades over 6 milliseconds at each cut, so you won't hear clicks.

Keep the pauses that matter

A pause before a punchline or after a question is part of the delivery. Every shortened pause appears as a ⏸ chip in the transcript; tap it to keep that pause whole. Choose “Keep all” to switch the silence cutter off and only cut filler words or words you pick.

Tips for a cleaner cut

  • Record in one take and don't stop the camera to think: the editor removes the thinking time for you.
  • Leave a second of silence before you start talking. It helps the detector learn your room's noise level.
  • Background music under speech makes pauses harder to find. Cut silence from the dry voice recording first, then add music in your main editor.
  • If a sentence ends too abruptly, switch from Tight to Natural rather than keeping pauses one by one.

Questions

Does removing silence also remove “um” and “uh”?

Yes, if “Cut filler words” is ticked (it is by default). The speech model marks hesitation sounds like “um”, “uh” and “er”, and they're cut along with the pause around them. Untick it to cut only silence.

Will it cut quiet speech by mistake?

It's tuned to treat anything clearly louder than the background as sound, and it never cuts a word the speech model heard. Whispered or very distant speech can still be mistaken for silence; look for a crossed-out pause where you were talking and tap it to keep it.

Does it work on screen recordings and podcasts?

Yes. Anything with a voice track works. For a podcast with two people, the pauses between speakers are shortened too, which tightens the conversation.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
