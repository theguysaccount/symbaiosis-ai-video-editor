Automatic editing works best on recordings that give it clear signals. A few habits while recording save more time than any setting afterwards.

## Before you speak

- **Record a second of silence first.** The silence detector learns your room's background level from the quietest parts of the recording. A clean second at the start makes that easy.
- **Turn off music and fans you control.** Steady noise is fine; noise that comes and goes (music, a TV) makes pauses look like sound.
- **Get the microphone close.** A clip-on or a phone held a hand's width away gives a big gap between your voice and the room, which is exactly what the detector measures.

## While you speak

- **Don't stop recording to think.** Pause, think, then repeat the sentence from the start. The pause will be cut; then select the flubbed take in the transcript and cut it too.
- **Restart whole sentences, not half-words.** "So the main, so the main point is" is easy to fix by cutting the first three words. A restart in the middle of a word is harder to cut cleanly.
- **Hold your position.** Jump cuts are less noticeable when your head is in roughly the same place before and after a pause. Look at the lens, not at your notes, when you resume.
- **Finish on a beat of silence.** It gives the cut at the end something to land on.

## After you stop

- **Review the transcript, not the timeline.** Read it like a script: anything you wouldn't write, cut.
- **Fix names before export.** The speech model doesn't know your product names; correct them once and the captions are right.
- **Export short-form and long-form separately.** One recording, two exports: Natural pacing and the original shape for YouTube; Tight pacing, 9:16 and captions for Shorts.
