Dead air is the most common reason viewers leave a talking video early. Cutting it by hand means scrubbing through the timeline, splitting clips and closing gaps, which takes longer than the recording itself. An automatic silence cutter does the scrubbing for you; your job is to check its work.

## The quick way

1. Open the [editor]({{root}}) and choose your video. MP4, MOV and WebM all work. Nothing is uploaded: the file is read by your browser.
2. Within a second or two, every long pause appears in the transcript as a crossed-out ⏸ chip, and the preview plays the edit with the pauses removed.
3. Choose the pacing: **Tight** for fast short-form, **Natural** for most talking heads, **Relaxed** for tutorials.
4. Play it through once. Tap any pause you want to keep, such as the beat before a punchline.
5. Press **Export video** and download the MP4.

## What "removing silence" should really mean

Deleting every quiet moment makes speech sound breathless and robotic. A good silence cutter **shortens** pauses instead: it keeps a sliver of quiet after the last word and before the next one. In Symbaiosis, Natural pacing turns any pause longer than half a second into a 0.2-second gap. That's close to the rhythm of fluent speech, so cuts are hard to hear.

It also matters *where* the cut lands. Cutting in the middle of a breath or the tail of a word produces a click. The editor moves every cut to the quietest moment nearby and fades the sound over 6 milliseconds at each edge.

## Silence and noise

"Silence" in a real recording is never zero: there's room tone, a fan, traffic. The detector measures your recording's own background level and treats anything close to it as quiet. That's why it works in a noisy room, as long as you are clearly louder than the background. If you recorded with music underneath your voice, pauses won't be quiet at all; cut the dry voice recording first and add music afterwards.

## Checking the result

Watch for two things in the preview:

- **A word that's clipped.** Very soft word endings ("help", "fact") can be mistaken for silence. Switch to Natural or Relaxed pacing, which leaves more room around words.
- **A pause that mattered.** Tap its ⏸ chip to keep it whole.

When it sounds right, export. You can return to the edit from the result screen and export again with different settings, as often as you like.
